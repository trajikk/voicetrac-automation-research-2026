01 · The Shift

Search stopped being ten blue links.

Buyers are asking AI assistants for recommendations directly — and getting one confident answer instead of a page of options to click through. If your brand isn't part of that answer, it doesn't matter how well you rank below it.

Old Search

  • – A ranked list of links, user picks one
  • – Optimized for keywords and backlinks
  • – You control the page, not the verdict
  • – Success = position on a results page

AI Search

  • + One synthesized answer, often no links at all
  • + Optimized for clarity, structure, and citations
  • + The model decides who gets named
  • + Success = being the brand it mentions

06 · FAQ

Common questions.

What is GEO / AI visibility, exactly? +
Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of making your brand easy for AI models to find, understand, and cite when they generate an answer — the equivalent of SEO for a search result that's a paragraph instead of a page of links.
How is this different from regular SEO? +
SEO optimizes for ranking algorithms and click-through. GEO optimizes for a language model deciding, in real time, which brands are trustworthy and specific enough to name. The two overlap, but ranking well on Google doesn't guarantee an AI model will mention you — we've seen both directions.
Do you build websites, or just optimize what's there? +
Both. If your site's foundation is solid, we optimize its structure and content for AI readability. If it isn't, we design and build a new one from scratch — either way, it ends up built to be read and cited by AI models, not just by people.
Which AI platforms do you track? +
Every scan checks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Copilot by default. We can add other platforms relevant to your industry on request.
Do we need to already rank well on Google? +
No. Strong traditional SEO helps, but it isn't a prerequisite — AI visibility and Google rank are related but separate. We've grown AI mentions for brands that weren't on the first page of Google at all.
