05 · FAQ

Common questions.

What does an engagement actually look like? +
It starts with an in-person or live audit of your current process, followed by a build phase where we design and implement the specific systems your team needs, then ongoing refinement as things change.
Do you replace our CRM, or work within it? +
Almost always we work within what you already have — the goal is to fix how it's used and connected, not force a migration. A replacement only comes up if the current tool is genuinely the bottleneck.
How long does a typical engagement run? +
The audit and initial build are the concentrated phase; after that most clients keep us on for ongoing refinement as the team and volume grow. Scope depends on how much of the process needs rebuilding.
Do you work with our existing sales team? +
Yes — we build with your team, not around them, and train reps directly on anything new so it actually gets used.
What industries do you work with? +
Any business with a defined sales process and a team selling it — the specific fixes vary by industry, but the audit approach is the same.
