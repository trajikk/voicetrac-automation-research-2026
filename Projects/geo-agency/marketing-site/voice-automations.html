01 · The Problem

A missed call is a lead that just called your competitor instead.

Most inbound calls happen outside a perfect moment — after hours, mid-lunch, three calls deep already. Voicemail doesn't qualify anyone, and by the time someone calls back, the moment that made them pick up the phone has usually passed.

Without Voice Automation

  • – Calls outside business hours go to voicemail
  • – Front desk juggles calls, walk-ins, and everything else at once
  • – Leads sit unqualified until someone has time to call back
  • – No consistent record of what was actually said

With Scanline

  • + Every call answered instantly, any hour, any volume
  • + Leads qualified and routed before a human ever picks up
  • + Appointments booked directly onto the calendar
  • + Full transcript and summary logged automatically

05 · FAQ

Common questions.

Will it sound robotic to customers? +
No — the goal is a natural conversation, not a phone tree. It's built and tuned specifically on how your business talks, including handling interruptions and follow-up questions.
Can it transfer to a real person? +
Yes. It's trained to recognize when a call needs a human — a complex issue, a frustrated caller, a request outside its scope — and hands off cleanly, with context already gathered.
What happens with a question it can't answer? +
It says so, takes a message or books a callback, and routes it to your team instead of guessing or making something up.
Are calls recorded? +
Yes — every call is recorded, transcribed, and summarized, so you have a full record for quality review and coaching.
Does it work with our existing phone system? +
Yes. We connect it to the number you already use — no new number to advertise, no changes to how customers reach you.
